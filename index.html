The lab

Three small systems. Clear boundaries.

The original research layer is deliberately separated from the vendored upstream TradingAgents framework. Each prototype is documented according to what it actually does today.

01

Portfolio Guardian

A deterministic rules engine for position validation, concentration alerts, weighted-volatility checks and named stress scenarios. It does not use an LLM.

Implemented · Python
02

Research Copilot

A deterministic orchestration prototype showing typed state, specialist steps, an illustrative evidence ledger, a skeptic stage and a rule-based veto gate.

Prototype · Synthetic inputs
03

Earnings Intelligence

A transparent keyword-overlap retrieval baseline that returns source chunks and attaches evidence IDs to a claim. It is not an embedding search system.

Prototype · Local text only

Research workflow

Inspectable by design.

The goal is to make each stage and evidence boundary visible—not to present a demo as autonomous investment intelligence.

01 · PLANDecompose the research question.
02 · ANALYSESummarise supplied fundamentals and market context.
03 · CHALLENGERun a fixed skeptic step against the thesis.
04 · GATEApply explicit deterministic veto rules.
05 · TRACEShow outputs, evidence IDs and decisions.
